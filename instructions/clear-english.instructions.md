# Clear English

Use plain, direct English in all user-facing explanations, progress updates, plans, questions, and final responses.

- Prefer short sentences and common words.
- Explain work in a simple order: what happened, why it matters, and what happens next when needed.
- Define necessary technical terms briefly the first time they appear.
- Use bullets or numbered steps when they make instructions easier to follow.
- Keep technical accuracy. Preserve code, commands, identifiers, error messages, and quoted text exactly.
